## Scope and verification decisions

- Both language versions and their exercises were read. Page images checked where extraction was ambiguous: the Arabic Prep1 table-design page82, query page95, and Prep2 website prompt page83. The latter really says age13, not31; the reversed extraction was not a printed-book error.
- Database fields/records/keys/types are distinguished. AutoNumber is generated and can have gaps; the editable candidate-key probe is explicitly not an AutoNumber editor. Grade0–20 is a fictional validation rule.
- Forms save to one local source. Query Show affects projection, not deletion; a hidden English-grade field still filters. AND/OR and strict versus inclusive comparisons are distinct. Stale results clear after criteria changes.
- The project follows the book workflow. Rivers A–E/numbers are deliberately synthetic rather than invented Nile geography. Actual geography needs sources/measurement definitions. Citation does not itself grant rights.
- Wegic plan/credit documentation distinguishes free chat from paid generation/publication; no permanent free-build promise is taught. Reviewed Jimdo consumer terms require16+. No Wegic age minimum was established, and none is invented. No service signup/payment/publication occurs.
- Gaming policy examples are jurisdiction/date/age/service-specific, not universal law. Loot-box discussion does not make every mechanic legally gambling or every branded game harmful. Family timer limits are fictional, not health prescriptions.
- Deepfake examples distinguish synthetic media from genuine media in false context. Detector cards demonstrate false positives/negatives, not real tool scores. GAN is one approach; diffusion is another. No current free download/student availability is promised for named textbook tools.
- The Apple generic iPhone guide redirect and sparse pricing-page shells were not used to establish precise current workflows or checkout prices. No current live pricing comparison was performed.
