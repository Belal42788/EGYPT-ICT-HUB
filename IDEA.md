EGYPT-ICT-HUB — A bilingual (Arabic/English) educational platform for Egyptian ICT & Computer Science students (preparatory & secondary stages). Created by Bilal Abdelnasser (ICT & CS instructor).
Structure: Stage → Year → Unit → Lesson. Each lesson is a single-file interactive HTML page with embedded CSS/JS — explanations, step-by-step practice, quizzes, and glossary.
Live: https://belal42788.github.io/EGYPT-ICT-HUB/
