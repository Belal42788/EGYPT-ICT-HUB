## Working visual mechanisms

- Digital service: animated request token, completeness and verified-route failures, a trackable fictional response, and paper-versus-digital workflow comparison. No real government claim or payment.
- OS: open/close two mock apps, allocate/release model memory, rename a dummy file with permission checks, detect an offline printer, and animate a mock print job. No actual device inspection or changes.
- Cyber: symbolic multi-source request load, overload and an explicitly idealised filter; fictional locked files and tested-backup recovery; inert social-engineering scenarios. No target URL, network attack, executable payload or real encryption.
- Big Data: structured, unstructured and semi-structured views; synthetic batches; duplicate and invalid-value filtering; correct clean total and mean; 5Vs tied to the observed operations. This is a small teaching sample, not actual big-data infrastructure.
- AI algorithms: sequence, sign including zero, classroom threshold-50 rule, five-iteration loop, search found/absent cases, animated adjacent swaps, and a tiny word-count model trained from labelled examples. Retraining changes learned counts. No claim that a hardcoded rule is ML, no confidence probabilities, no real spam protection or external AI.
- Strings/lists: Unicode code-point boxes and negative indexes; membership, uppercase copy and immutable-string handling; list append, first-match removal and absent-value errors; guarded empty-name filtering; fixed fictional composition-criteria samples, never a real password test.
