# Development of Information Technology

How Does Society Develop with Technology?

From Hunting to AI

### What will you learn in this lesson?

Today we will learn how human society developed through the ages — from the hunter-gatherer society to the information society — then discover the latest technologies like AI and the Internet of Things, and how technology changed our lives and created new concepts like technostress and the digital divide.

Stages of Societal Development

4 main stages: hunting → agriculture → industry → information.

Modern Technologies

Big Data, AI, Machine Learning, and IoT.

Its Impact on Our Lives

New concepts: technostress, technophobia, and the digital divide.

### ⚠️ Common Mistakes

Confusing the agrarian and industrialized societies — the first depends on farming and harvesting, the second on machines and mass production.

Thinking technology started with computers — no, technology has existed since the beginning of humanity, but its form changed across the ages.

Flow of Societal Development

From Hunting to Information

### Click each stage to learn its details:

### Stage 1 — Hunter-gatherer society

From the birth of humanity, people lived through hunting. The technologies were: stone axes, bows and arrows, fire-starting techniques, and language.

### Stage 2 — Agrarian society

From around 13,000 BCE, people lived a stable life by growing and harvesting food. The technologies: agricultural tools and irrigation.

### Stage 3 — Industrialized society

From the mid-18th century (post-industrial revolution), industrialization advanced through mass production using machinery. The technologies: steam engines, industrial machinery, and printing.

### Stage 4 — Information society

From the latter half of the 20th century, automation was promoted by the advancement of computers. The technologies: computers and the internet.

### Society 5.0

A new society where virtual space (cyberspace) and real space (physical space) are integrated to solve various social issues and achieve economic development.

### ⚠️ Common Mistakes

Mixing up the order of the stages — the correct order is: hunter-gatherer ← agrarian ← industrialized ← information.

Thinking the information society started with the internet — no, it started in the latter half of the 20th century with the advancement of computers.

New Information Technologies

Technologies Attracting Attention

### Click each technology to understand it:

Big Data

Vast collections of data in various forms, with different characteristics and of various types. Data consists of three elements: volume, variety, and generation/update frequency.

💡 Volume + variety + constant updates = the essential characteristics of Big Data.

AI (Artificial Intelligence)

A technology that uses computers to artificially mimic human intellectual behavior.

💡 Examples: autonomous driving, telemedicine, smart agriculture, and smart homes.

Machine Learning

A technology where computers learn from large amounts of data to extract rules and patterns from the data.

IoT (Internet of Things)

Technology that supports daily life by connecting household appliances and other items to the internet, thereby allowing the appliances to communicate with each other.

Smart Home

A residence that utilizes IoT and AI technologies to provide a more comfortable living environment.

VR (Virtual Reality)

A technology that allows users to experience an illusion of reality in a simulated environment.

AR (Augmented Reality)

A technology that uses computer processing to enhance the real-world environment perceived by human senses (vision, etc.).

### ⚠️ Common Mistakes

Confusing VR and AR — VR takes you into a fully simulated environment, while AR adds information to the real environment.

Confusing AI and Machine Learning — AI is the overall mimicking of human behavior, while Machine Learning is a specific method where machines learn from data.

Changes in Life Caused by IT

The Other Side of Technology

### Click each concept to understand it:

Technostress

The overall term for mental and physical problems that arise from using computers.

Technophobia

A state of anxiety or fear towards using computers due to a sense of discomfort or lack of confidence in handling them.

Techno-Addiction

Excessive dependency on computers.

VDT Disorder

Shoulder stiffness and eye strain caused by prolonged computer use.

Digital Divide

The gap between those who can utilize information technology and those who cannot.

### ⚠️ Common Mistakes

Confusing technostress and technophobia — technostress is mental and physical problems, while technophobia is a state of anxiety about using computers.

Confusing the digital divide and techno-addiction — the divide is the inability to use technology, while addiction is excessive dependency on it.
