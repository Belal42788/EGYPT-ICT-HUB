# Block Breaker Development Presentation

What are we learning today?

Today we present the Block Breaker game we developed in the previous lessons: game type, development stories, and collaboration with AI.

### Game type

We present the game title and rules clearly.

### Development stories

We tell about our creativity, strategies, and difficulties we faced.

### Collaboration with AI

We explain what AI can do and what it cannot do.

Common mistakes

Memorizing and reading from the slide — presenting should be explaining and communicating.

Ignoring the required presentation points (game type, development stories, collaboration with AI).

Game type

We start the presentation by defining the game: its title, and its rules.

Game Title: e.g. Block Breaker.

Game Rules: the ball bounces off the paddle, blocks break, you have lives.

Explain how the player wins or loses.

Common mistakes

Explaining the rules vaguely without examples.

Forgetting what makes the player win or lose.


### Questions

Correct Answer: A

Q: What must we clarify about the rules?
Options:
A. that it is hard
B. how to play and how to win or lose
C. rules aren't important
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Correct! How to play and how the player wins or loses.

Development stories

We tell how we developed the game: the creative points (strategies) and difficulties we faced.

Creative points: strategies like changing ball speed or score color at a high number.

Difficulties: like adding more blocks made the game too difficult.

How you overcame each difficulty — that makes the story stronger.

Common mistakes

Telling every tiny detail instead of focusing on the most important creations and difficulties.

Forgetting to tell how you overcame the difficulty — important in the presentation.

Q: An example of creative points?
Options:
A. the program stopped
B. the game title
C. changing ball speed / score color
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Correct! Changing ball speed or score color.

Q: In development stories we tell what?
Options:
A. creativity, difficulties, and how we overcame them
B. colors only
C. rules only
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Correct! Creativity, difficulties, and how we overcame them.

Collaboration with AI

We explain in the presentation what AI can do, and what it cannot do.

What AI can do: writing code fast, new ideas, summarizing.

What AI cannot do: truly understanding the project, making decisions, checking quality.

We explain how you and AI worked together.

Common mistakes

Imagining AI truly understands like a human — we are the ones who understand and decide.

Not mentioning your role in the process — collaboration means work from both sides.

Q: Something AI can do?
Options:
A. truly understanding the project
B. writing code fast
C. making the final decision
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Correct! Writing code fast and new ideas.

Q: Something AI cannot do?
Options:
A. writing code fast
B. giving new ideas
C. understanding the project and making decisions
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Correct! Understanding the project, making decisions, and checking quality.
