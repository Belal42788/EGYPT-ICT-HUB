# Information Ethics

What is Information Ethics?

Core Definition

Information ethics is the foundational concepts and attitudes necessary for conducting appropriate activities in the information society. This is regardless of the presence or absence of laws.

### ⚠️ Common Mistakes

Thinking ethics only means laws — no, ethics often comes before laws.

Thinking one small mistake is no big deal — even small errors can grow if spread quickly.

Thinking anonymity protects you from all responsibility — no, even anonymous actions have real impact.

Disseminating Information Online

Choices & Risks

### Characteristics of Information on the Internet

Information on the internet has two important characteristics:

### 1. Easy to spread

Information spreads incredibly fast — one click and the whole world can see it. No geographic limits.

### 2. Hard to disappear

Even if you delete a post or photo, someone may have screenshotted or saved it before you did. Information doesn't die easily.

### Infringement of Others' Rights

You must know there are limits when dealing with other people's content:

Copyright

You must not share images or videos owned by others without permission — even if taken from a public site.

Personal Information

You must not leak another person's personal information (name, address, phone number) on the internet.

Privacy

You must not infringe on others' privacy — even if they don't know you're doing it.

Online Bullying

You must not post comments that slander or defame others or engage in online bullying.

### Geotagging — your location revealed from a photo!

Geotagging is location information (latitude and longitude) embedded in photos and videos taken with smartphones. There is a risk of being identified, such as having your home pinpointed based on the location where the photo was taken.

### Disinformation and Rumors

Disinformation and rumors: false information that is deliberately spread or baseless rumors. Problems that occur with smartphones and social media.

### ⚠️ Common Mistakes

Thinking deleting a post is enough — no, someone may have saved or screenshot it.

Thinking geotagging is always a good feature — no, it can be a major security risk.

Sharing any news you see without verifying — even if from someone with many followers.

Smartphone & Social Media Problems

Digital Challenges

### What is Social Media?

Social media : Services that provide platforms where individuals can connect with each other on the internet.

### Types of Digital Problems

Click each type to learn what it is and how to protect yourself:

Internet Addiction

A state where one becomes excessively immersed in the internet to the extent of interfering with daily life.

💡 Solution: set a daily time limit, take offline breaks, and practice physical hobbies.

Using Smartphone While Walking

The act of using a smartphone while walking — can cause accidents and prevent you from seeing the path.

💡 Solution: don't use your phone while walking in the street. Stop in a safe spot if you need to call or reply.

Cybercrime

The misuse of computers and networks for criminal activities.

💡 Solution: don't open suspicious links, don't download files from untrusted sources, and use strong passwords.

Identity Theft

The act of a third party pretending to be a certain individual or organization to steal IDs or passwords.

💡 Solution: never share your personal info with anyone, and verify the identity of who you're talking to.

Leakage of Personal Information

When personal information that should remain confidential is disclosed to a third party.

💡 Solution: share your info cautiously, use privacy settings, and stay aware of what you post.

### ⚠️ Common Mistakes

Thinking social media is always dangerous — no, it's useful when used properly.

Sharing personal info to win prizes or deals — these are common scam methods.

Thinking you're not targeted because you're young — no, children and teens are among the most targeted groups.
