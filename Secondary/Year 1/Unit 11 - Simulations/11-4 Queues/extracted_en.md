The Queue: Waiting for a Service

A queue is a line formed when customers wait for a service, like a supermarket register or a theme park attraction, and we calculate service times and waiting time from it.

We represent queue changes in a chart: the vertical axis is the number of customers in line, the horizontal axis is time, black is service time and gray is waiting time.

What Is a Queue?

A queue is a line formed when customers wait for a service, such as at a supermarket register or an attraction at a theme park.

Calculating Service Times

Service start time: when there are no customers at the register it is the time when the customer arrives, and when there is a previous customer it is when the previous customer finished. Service end time = service start + service duration, and waiting time = service start − arrival time.

Service Times

Start time

No queue = arrival; with queue = previous customer finished

End time

Start time + service duration

Waiting time

Start time − arrival time

Waiting Time and Queue Length

Each customer waiting time = service start − arrival time, the longest waiting customer is the one with the largest waiting time, and the queue length at a moment is the number of customers in line, with its duration being the period the queue stayed at its maximum.
