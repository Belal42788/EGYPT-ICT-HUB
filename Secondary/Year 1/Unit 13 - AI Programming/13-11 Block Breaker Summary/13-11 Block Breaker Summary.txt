In this lesson, you will prepare to present the block breaker game you created in the previous class.

Warm Up
Reflect on the block breaker app you created in the last lesson and write down your thoughts on the following 
points.
1) What kind of game did you create?
• Game Title
• Game Rules
Examples:
Number of lives, rankings, presence of items, etc.
2) Development Stories
• Creative Points (Strategies used)
Examples:
I made the ball speed change every time it hits the player.
I made the score text color change when a high score is reached.
• Difficulties
Examples:
Increasing the number of blocks made the game too difficult.
The program stopped working halfway through, so I had to start over from scratch.
3) Collaboration with AI
• What AI can do.
• What AI cannot do.
You may use AI to help you think about the points above.
