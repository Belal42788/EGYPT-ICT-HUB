# Block Breaker Summary

What are we learning today?

Today we prepare the presentation of the Block Breaker game we made: we reflect on the game, write our creativity and difficulties, and organize them into 4-5 slides.

### Reflect on the game

We write our thoughts: game type and rules, creativity, and difficulties.

### Collaboration with AI

We define what AI can do and what it cannot do.

### Organized presentation

We summarize our work into 4-5 slides and practice presenting.

Common mistakes

Forgetting to document your creativity and difficulties while working — documentation helps you in the presentation.

Making the presentation longer than needed — only 4-5 slides are required.

Reflect on the game

We reflect on the Block Breaker game we made last lesson, and write our thoughts on three points.

Game type: title and rules (lives, score, items...).

Development stories: creative points (strategies) and difficulties.

Collaboration with AI: what it can do and what it cannot.

You may use AI to help you think about these points. Example creativity: I made the ball speed change every time it hits the player. Example difficulty: increasing the number of blocks made the game too difficult.

Common mistakes

Claiming the game worked perfectly the first time — difficulties are part of the real story.

Confusing what AI did and what you did — you must separate them clearly.

Organize the presentation

We organize what we wrote into a presentation of 4 to 5 slides. We may use AI to help us summarize.

Slide 1: title, game type, and rules.

Slide 2: creative points and strategies.

Slide 3: difficulties and how you overcame them.

Slide 4: what AI can do and cannot do.

Slide 5: summary and conclusion.

Common mistakes

Putting everything on one slide — spread it over 4-5 slides.

Copying long text verbatim — a slide should be a short summary.
