# About Generative AI

What are we learning today?

Today we learn about Generative AI — what it is, how it works, and how to use it well to save time in study and work.

### What is Generative AI

A smart tool that does intellectual tasks for you like generating ideas and summarizing texts.

### How it works

It chooses the most likely next word after what we type in the Prompt.

### Hallucination

It may say wrong things, so we must verify info and not trust it 100%.

Common mistakes

Thinking the AI knows things for sure — no, it only picks a likely word.

Trusting its answer 100% without checking it.

How does Generative AI work?

Generative AI has learned from vast amounts of information worldwide. It generates natural text by selecting “the word most likely to come next” after what the user writes in the Prompt (instruction).

The user writes a Prompt, and the AI selects the most likely next word, building the sentence gradually.

Example: when you ask “What is the capital of Egypt?” it selects “Cairo” as the most likely word after “The capital of Egypt is”, and builds the sentence “The capital of Egypt is Cairo.”

Hallucination

The key point is that Generative AI does not “know” as a fact that Cairo is the capital of Egypt; it merely picks the highest-probability word. So it doesn't always give the right answer, and due to its mechanism it is “certain to say wrong things with some probability”. This is called Hallucination.

Common mistakes

Taking the AI's answer as fact without checking it.

Thinking every answer it gives is correct — no, there's a chance it's wrong.

Not using a search engine to verify the info it said.
