Lesson introduction — methods of communication on the internet

Today we'll talk about the ways we communicate on the internet — how we send and receive information without getting lost, and how to choose the right medium for each situation.

We'll learn the famous ways like email, instant messaging, video calls and blogs, and see the platforms where people meet like social media and video sharing. Finally we'll understand the shared characteristics that set internet communication apart from any other communication.

Think of it this way:

before choosing a way to communicate, ask yourself: do I need to reach one specific person or many? do I need an instant reply or am I not in a hurry? The answer decides your choice.

### ⚠️ Common Mistakes

Mixing up CC and BCC — both send to the maximum number of people, but BCC is hidden.

Thinking anonymity on the internet means you can write anything — no, records are kept and your words may be misunderstood, so be respectful and accurate with your information.

The Concept of Email

the basic definition — book page 50

Email (Electronic mail): a tool for sending and receiving text-based messages over the internet. It has a feature called broadcast capability that allows the user to send messages to multiple recipients.

Simply: instead of writing a letter and sending it by post, you type a message on your computer or phone and it reaches the person in seconds no matter how far away. If you want to reach many people, you type all their addresses and hit send — each one gets the message. Imagine a teacher who wants to send homework to the whole class: instead of sending 30 messages, they send just one. You can also attach photos, videos and files, so email is not limited to text.

To:

you type the address(es) of the recipient(s) you want to send the message to directly.

CC:

you type the addresses of recipients you want to share the email content with, and all recipients see the CC addresses.

BCC:

like CC for sharing, but without the other recipients seeing the addresses or even knowing BCC is used.

Think of it this way:

To is the main home of the message, CC is like project members who see each other, and BCC is like a secret copy that reaches its people without anyone knowing.

Bulletin board system (BBS)

A public message board: you post a message, others see it and reply, and all the replies are visible to everyone

A BBS is like a digital notice board — you write a message, people see it and reply to it, and all the replies pile up under the message. One of the oldest forms of online communities before modern social media.

The name describes the idea: Bulletin means an announcement, and Board means a board — literally a public notice board that everyone can see.

A practical example: a questions forum — a student asks a question, another student replies, and the replies appear in sequence.

Tell them apart:

the BBS is public and open to everyone, while email is private and personal between a sender and a receiver.

Messaging app

instant exchange of text, photos and videos

Messaging apps like WhatsApp, LINE and SMS let us exchange text, photos and videos in seconds — for individuals or for groups. Their advantage is that communication is fast and direct, and it arrives instantly if the other side is online.

The official concept name is Messaging app: tools that enable exchanging text, photos and videos. Examples from the book: SMS (Short Message Service) and LINE.

Example / illustration:

the family group — everyone sends photos and videos, and everyone sees them and comments.

Video call

a conversation with sound and picture at the same moment

Video call: a tool that enables a real-time conversation while watching the other person's video over the internet.

A video call lets you talk to someone while seeing their face at the same moment over the internet — it combines sound and picture together. You need a camera, a microphone and a good internet connection.

A quick example:

a video call with your friend in another country — you see each other and talk at the same time.

The Blog is a personal website where you write like a diary — days, thoughts, articles — and publish it to people. The word itself is merged from web + log (a record). There is the blogger who picks a specific topic and writes about it regularly.

Example: a cooking blog — every week I publish a new recipe, and readers read it and interact.

Understand well:

a blog is Asynchronous — the post is written and read at different times, not an instant conversation.

Social platforms & Communication characteristics

Social media and Video sharing platform + the four communication characteristics

### Platforms

Social media: services providing platforms where individuals can connect with each other on the internet.

Simply: a platform like Facebook, Twitter (X) and Instagram — each person makes an account, builds a friend network, posts, likes and interacts with others. Information moves from everyone to everyone here, and anyone in the network shares and receives.

Examples:

Facebook, Twitter (X), Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn.

Video sharing platform: a service that allows a large, unspecified audience to share and watch videos posted by other unspecified users on the internet.

Simply: a platform like YouTube — any user posts a video, and a huge worldwide audience can watch it at any time. The video is a recorded broadcast that stays available to everyone.

Examples:

YouTube, Vimeo.

Get the difference:

Social media is interactive platforms for connecting with people — video sharing is a platform for broadcasting recorded videos to a large, unspecified audience.
