Lesson introduction — Network Transfer Speed

When you download a file or watch a video, you notice the size and the time are related. Today we learn the transfer speed and how to calculate it, its units bps, kbps, Mbps, and Gbps, the relationship between speed, time, and data volume, and the transfer efficiency.

Think of it this way:

Transfer speed is like the amount of water passing through a pipe per second — the wider the pipe, the faster the data reaches.

### ⚠️ Common Mistakes

Confusing the bit with the Byte — there are 8 bits in 1 Byte, and the speed unit bps is written in bits, not bytes.

Forgetting that the actual transfer speed = communication speed × transfer efficiency — so if efficiency is 80%, the actual speed is less than the advertised number.

Transfer Speed

Its unit is bps — bit per second.

Units of Measurement

converting units from smallest to largest — book page 109

1 kbps = 1,000 bps, 1 Mbps = 1,000 kbps, and 1 Gbps = 1,000 Mbps.

Each larger unit is 1,000 times the previous one — from bit to kilobit to megabit to gigabit.

Think of it this way:

Like meter and kilometer: a kilobit is like a kilometer, and a megabit is like a thousand kilometers — all multiples of a thousand.

Speed, Time, and Data Volume

three formulas that tie them together — book page 109

Transfer speed [bps] = data volume [bits] ÷ transfer time [seconds].

Transfer time [seconds] = data volume [bits] ÷ transfer speed [bps].

Data volume [bits] = transfer speed [bps] × transfer time [seconds].

Think of it this way:

Like distance, speed, and time: knowing two lets you find the third — distance is data volume, speed is transfer speed, and time is transfer time.

three formulas, one triangle

speed = volume ÷ time

time = volume ÷ speed

volume = speed × time

Transfer Efficiency

the ratio at which communication is achieved — book page 109

Transfer efficiency is the ratio at which a certain communication speed can be achieved.

Actual transfer speed = communication speed [bps] × transfer efficiency.

Think of it this way:

If the internet speed says 100 Mbps and the efficiency is 80%, the actual speed is 80 Mbps — it will not reach the advertised number exactly.

from communication speed to actual speed

transfer efficiency — the achievement ratio

actual speed = communication speed × efficiency

Worked Examples

applying the formulas to problems — book page 109

Example: calculate the time to transfer 20 MB at 40 Mbps with 100% efficiency. Transfer time = (20 × 1,000 × 1,000 × 8) ÷ (40 × 1,000 × 1,000) = 4 seconds.

Example: calculate the time to transfer 1 GB at 100 Mbps with 80% efficiency. Actual speed = 100 × 0.8 = 80 Mbps, and transfer time = (1 × 1,000 × 1,000 × 1,000 × 8) ÷ (80 × 1,000 × 1,000) = 100 seconds.

Think of it this way:

When converting MB to bits you multiply by 1,000 × 1,000 × 8 — then divide by the speed in bps.

What data volume in bytes can be transmitted in one minute at 30 bps?

A  225 bytes

B  180 bytes

C  30 bytes

✅ Answer: A — 225 bytes

30 bps × 60 seconds = 1,800 bits, and 1,800 ÷ 8 = 225 bytes.

What data volume in MB can be transmitted in five minutes at 10 Mbps?

A  50 MB

B  375 MB

C  10 MB

✅ Answer: B — 375 MB

5 minutes = 300 seconds, and the volume = 10 × 1,000 × 1,000 × 300 ÷ (8 × 1,000 × 1,000) = 375 MB.
